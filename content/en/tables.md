## Usage and Attribution

Equipment and I/O lists document device/signal planning; alarm and archive sheets document configuration. Their presence does not establish runtime acceptance. Relationships to the current simulation and field variants appear in [variant documentation](versions.md), with scope in [evidence E05](evidence.md#e05).

Original worksheet content is not translated or rewritten. Both language editions link to the same Excel copies. Only public-author and local-save-path metadata is processed; worksheets, formulas and formatting are preserved. See [S14](sources.md#s14). Previews use stored values; native Excel recalculation was not performed.
