## Process understanding

The system transfers powder from a low-level feed point through three successive screw conveyors, S01–S03. Elevator T01 raises the material to the bin-top level, where distributor C01 directs it to one of three storage bins, A01–A03. Fan F01 supports dust collection along the conveying system.

The reference diagram specifies manual/automatic operation for individual devices, automatic interlocked starting, successful C01 selection before the conveyor line starts, and F01 starting in advance of the line. These requirements define control targets; they do not by themselves demonstrate field performance or dust-control compliance.

The prose above organizes the author’s original note and supplied process diagram. The reference diagram is not presented as a drawing authored in this website revision.
